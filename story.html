Our Heritage

Hand-Pressed with Persistence

It began in the rolling hills of Val d'Orcia, where Nonna Bianca spent her mornings at the communal stone mill. She believed that the heat of industrial grinding stole the "anima"—the soul—of the grain.

She taught us that the secret to the perfect al dente bite wasn't just the recipe, but the patience. We still use her original brass dies, each bearing the marks of a thousand Sunday dinners.

Honoring the Land

We source from people, not corporations. Every bag of flour is traceable back to the patch of earth where it basked in the Mediterranean sun.

The Molino Silla Mill

Located in the Umbrian valley, this water-powered mill has been cold-pressing our ancient grains since the 1800s, preserving the vital nutrients and nutty aromas.
